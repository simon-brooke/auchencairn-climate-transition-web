**Update on PVG and safeguarding**

Under new regulations anyone working with children needs to have Protection of Vulnerable Groups checks and certification. This includes people doing e.g. bike bus escort. Reports about safeguarding concerns with any of our employees or volunteers should be made in writing to Lee. We have a safeguarding policy, which Lee is updating to bring into accord with the new regulations.
